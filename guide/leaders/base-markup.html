Base Markup Tool

Base Markup turns any screenshot into an attack plan — draw paths, drop troop icons, number your attack order, and share it without leaving the app.

Get to it two ways: tap the brush icon on a base row in War Planner to mark up that specific opponent base, or open it as a standalone tool from your Dashboard for any image — recon shots, your clan mates' bases, anything.

Pinch to zoom up to 8x and pan with two fingers — drawing only triggers with one finger, so zooming never accidentally adds a stray line. Draw arrows, dots, stars, freehand lines, highlights, or text labels in five colours, and undo or redo as many steps as you need.

Tip: The troops tool drops recognizable icons for any of 83 troops, spells, and sieges — grouped by Heroes, Pets, Air, Ground, Spells, and Sieges — so "send the Super Wizard here" doesn't need a caption.

The numbered attack-order tool auto-increments with each tap, so you can mark a 1-2-3 funnel without manually typing numbers. When you're done, export straight to your phone's share sheet, or use the Post to Discord button to send the image and an optional caption directly to your clan's webhook — no bot, no login.
