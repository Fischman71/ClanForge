Member Analytics & Leader Dashboard

The Leader Dashboard is the spreadsheet you've been keeping by hand — except it updates itself. One row per member, every stat that actually matters for running a clan.

Open the Leader Dashboard from the Leader Tools hub on your Dashboard. Each row is a member — join date and join order, role, town hall, league, war preference, and a running set of activity columns: classic war stars and 0-attacks, CWL stars / 0-attacks / bonus count, Clan Games points, and Capital Raid loot. Tap any column header to sort, and tap a row to open that member's Player Details.

Time-window pills at the top control every activity column at once. Lifetime is the default; switch to Most Recent for the latest completed war or CWL season, or pick a custom date range to audit a specific stretch.

Tip: The on-break flag and shared notes column are the same data your whole leadership team sees and edits from Player Details — set a note once, everyone leading the clan sees it here.

Switch to Player view to flip the table — one row per time window (Lifetime plus recent months) for a single member you pick, useful for spotting a slow decline or confirming someone's been consistent before a promotion.
