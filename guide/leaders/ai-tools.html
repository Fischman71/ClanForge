AI Tools: Digest, Announcements & Description

Three AI-assisted tools live under AI Tools and Announcements — a leadership briefing, a member-facing announcement composer, and a clan description rewriter. All three are Pro, and all three show a one-time privacy note before first use.

The Weekly Leader's Digest is your private analytical briefing — pick Discord, WhatsApp, or Plain text format, optionally add context in the "Concerns" box, and ClanForge drafts a Health / Anomalies / Trends / Action Items summary from the clan's last 7 days. It factors in on-break flags so someone you've already marked as away doesn't show up as a red flag.

AI Announcements is the member-facing side — Free Write turns your rough note into a polished, format-aware post; Upcoming Events pulls straight from the events calendar; and Weekly Digest drafts a member-friendly recap of donations, war results, and roster changes. Clan mail and chat formats respect Clash of Clans' 230-character limit, splitting longer chat messages into multiple blocks automatically.

Tip: Everything generated is a draft. Edit the text before sharing — the AI writes in your clan's tone based on the tone chips you pick, but the final wording is your call.

The Clan Description Rewriter works the same way — pick a tone (Competitive, Casual, Recruiting, Funny), get three variants, tap to copy. It deliberately leaves out your clan's name and level so the copy stays reusable as those change.
