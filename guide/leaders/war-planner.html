War Planner

War Planner is where you turn the opponent roster into a real plan — assign bases, pick callers, leave notes, and let the whole clan see it the moment you save.

Open War Planner from the Wars tab once your opponent roster loads. Each opposing base gets its own row — tap one to assign a clan member, pick a caller, or jot a note for whoever's attacking it.

The scout bar above the base list gives you quick links to the opponent's Clan Detail, their war history, and a one-tap share of the full plan. Every change you make saves instantly and pushes to members in real time — no refresh needed on either end.

Tip: Tap the brush icon on any base row to open it in the Base Markup Tool and draw an attack path before sharing — covered in its own article.

Running CWL? Each round gets its own planner, same layout but themed crimson to keep it visually distinct from classic wars. If your clan prefers members picking their own targets, turn on self-call from Settings — members claim bases themselves instead of waiting on you.
