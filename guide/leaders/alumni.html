Departure Tracking & Alumni

When someone leaves your clan, ClanForge notices within a minute — and gives you a place to record why, so you know who's welcome back and who isn't.

The Members screen shows an "N departures" banner whenever someone's left since you last checked. As a leader or co-leader, tap a row to record why they left — pick from nine reasons, from "left no reason" to "kicked, banned", and add a 200-character note if you want more context.

Every classified departure feeds the Alumni screen, under Leader Tools. It groups every player who's ever left your clan into four buckets — Unclassified, Welcome back, Allow back, Banned — colour-coded by severity. Tap a row to jump to that player's Player Details, or tap the reason chip to reclassify.

Tip: If someone leaves and comes back, ClanForge shows a "Previously left this clan" card on their Player Details with the date, reason, and your note — handy context before you approve a re-invite.

Departure detection runs server-side on a sub-minute cron, so the banner and Alumni list stay current even if nobody in leadership has the app open when it happens.
