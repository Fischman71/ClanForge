Reading Your Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first thing you see — your clan's live snapshot plus quick links to everything else. Here's what's on it, where the numbers come from, and how often they update.

At the top, your clan's badge, name, and tag sit beside a stat strip — member count, war league, and current win streak. Tap the header to jump into Clan Detail for the full roster, town hall spread, and war record.

Below that, a small "Updated Xm ago" pill tells you when ClanForge last pulled fresh data from Clash of Clans. Clan info refreshes on its own roughly every 10 minutes — you don't need to do anything.

Tip: Pull down on the Dashboard to force a refresh. ClanForge checks the server first and only fetches new data if it's actually due — so spamming refresh won't get you rate-limited.

The Tools section is your jumping-off point — Builder Tracker for your timers, AI Tools and other leader features if you're a co-leader or leader (a small gold dot marks anything Pro-only), and a Discord link for support. If you've verified more than one player tag, an Accounts row above the tools lets you flip between them without leaving the screen.
