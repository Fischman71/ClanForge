Your War Assignments

When your clan's in war prep or actively fighting, the Wars screen shows your assignment right at the top — which base to hit, any notes from your leader, and when it was last changed.

Open the Wars tab during preparation or battle day and look for the "Your assignment(s)" card. It lists the opponent base number(s) your leader or co-leader wants you on, plus any notes — call order, hero levels to watch for, what to bring.

Assignments update in real time — if your leader changes your base mid-war, the card refreshes on its own with an "Edited by [name] · [time] ago" line so you know it's current. Members and elders see this card read-only; only leaders and co-leaders can edit it.

Tip: Some clans run self-call instead — if your leader has it on, you'll claim your own base from the opponent roster rather than waiting for an assignment.

If you've still got an unused attack with about an hour left on the clock, ClanForge sends a local reminder notification — no excuses for a missed hit.
