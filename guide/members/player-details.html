Player Details and Stats

Player Details is your personal stat sheet — current numbers, this season's totals, and trends built from your in-game achievements. Tap any member's name (including your own) to open it.

The header shows your name, player tag (long-press to copy), XP level, and league. Below it, a stat grid covers war stars, lifetime Capital Gold, time in your current clan, town hall level, builder base, and war preference.

The "This Season" tiles cover attack wins, defense wins, and trophy counts for both villages. Below that, ClanForge tracks 13 metrics — donations, Capital Gold, war stars, Clan Games points, and more — as rate-of-change trends, not just totals.

Tip: Those trend charts work by comparing your in-game achievement totals (which only ever go up) between snapshots. No guesswork — just your actual numbers, sampled over time.

Further down, a War History card shows your classic and CWL attack record, including a running count of times you didn't use an attack. If you've left and rejoined the clan, a "Previously left this clan" card shows when and why — leaders can set this from the same screen.
