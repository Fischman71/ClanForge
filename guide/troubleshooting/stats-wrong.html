Stats look wrong

Most "wrong" numbers are just behind — ClanForge refreshes clan and member data on a cache cycle, not instantly on every in-game change. A pull-to-refresh and a minute usually sorts it.

Clan and member data is fetched server-side on a rolling cycle, so a donation, trophy change, or war result can take a few minutes to appear. Pull down on the Dashboard, Members, or Tracking screen to force a fresh pull rather than waiting out the cycle.

Tip: Season and Lifetime totals (war stars, CWL stars, Clan Games, raid loot) are calculated from snapshots taken over time, not a single live number. Right after install, or for a player who's been inactive, there may not be enough snapshots yet to show a clean delta — give it a few days of normal use and the numbers fill in.

On the free tier, history older than 30 days is downsampled to save space — you'll see fewer data points further back, not incorrect ones. Pro keeps full resolution indefinitely. If a number still looks wrong after a refresh and a day of normal play, it's worth a report — see Crashes & how to report for where to send it.
