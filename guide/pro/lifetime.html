Lifetime pass details

One payment, no renewals, ever. Lifetime unlocks the entire Pro feature set — Clan Family, 5-account multi-account, hosted AI tools, full-resolution history, pushed war assignments — for $49.99, capped at 250 spots.

Feature-for-feature, Lifetime is identical to Pro Yearly. The only difference is how you pay: Yearly renews at $39.99 every 12 months, Lifetime is a single $49.99 charge and you're done. Anything Pro unlocks today, Lifetime includes — and anything Pro unlocks in the future is included too, automatically, no upgrade fee.

Tip: The counter on /pricing shows how many Lifetime spots are left. Once all 250 are claimed, the pass is gone for good — Monthly and Yearly stay available as normal.

Lifetime is tied to your primary account, same as any other Pro plan — it follows your first verified tag across adds, switches, and reinstalls. There's nothing to manage day-to-day: no billing cycle, no renewal date, no card on file to worry about.
