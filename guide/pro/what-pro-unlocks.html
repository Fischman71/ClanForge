What Pro unlocks

The free tier is the full member experience — you don't need Pro to use ClanForge. Pro is the leadership layer: more clans, more accounts, AI tools, and full history, all server-enforced the moment you subscribe.

Five things change with Pro. Clan Family goes from 0 to 5 linked clans, so you can run feeders, sisters, and minis as one family. Multi-account goes from 2 verified tags to 5. The AI Tools screen unlocks — Weekly Leader's Digest, Announcements composer, and the Clan Description rewriter all run on hosted Claude, no API key required. Snapshot history stops downsampling at 30 days and stays full-resolution indefinitely. And War Planner assignments push to members' phones in real time instead of staying view-only.

Tip: Pro is tied to your primary account — the first tag you ever verified — and travels with you across adds, switches, and reinstalls. The full feature-by-feature comparison table lives on /pricing.

Three ways to get there: Monthly at $3.99, Yearly at $39.99 (two months free), or Lifetime at $49.99 — a one-time payment capped at 250 spots. Or skip the card entirely: joining the Discord hands you 30 days of Pro to try the whole leadership suite before you commit.
