Managing your subscription

Monthly and Yearly Pro run through PayPal's standard subscription billing. Cancelling, checking your renewal date, and updating payment details all happen on PayPal's side — ClanForge just reads the result.

To cancel: open PayPal, go to Settings → Payments → Manage automatic payments, and cancel the ClanForge subscription. Your Pro access stays active until the end of the period you already paid for — no proration, no partial refunds, and no action needed inside the app. The next time ClanForge checks your status after that date, it'll quietly fall back to the free tier.

Tip: Lifetime has nothing to manage — it's a single payment with no recurring charge, so there's no subscription to cancel and nothing to appear in PayPal's automatic payments list.

Want to switch from Monthly to Yearly (or back)? Cancel the current plan in PayPal, then start the new one from /pricing — your Pro access carries through as long as the new subscription starts before the old one lapses. If a charge looks wrong or Pro didn't activate after payment, drop your PayPal receipt ID in Discord or open a GitHub issue — these edge cases get handled manually.
