Adding a second clan (Pro)

Clan Family links your feeders, sisters, minis, academies, or war allies into one flat group of up to five clans. Linked leaders get shared visibility into rosters, wars, and CWL — without merging clans or accounts.

Open Settings → Clan Family and use Request a Link: pick the relationship (feeder, sister, mini, academy, or war ally), enter the parent clan's tag, and send. The parent clan's leader or co-leader sees it under Incoming Requests and can accept, reject, or reject-and-block.

Tip: Pro is required on both sides — your subscription, or any verified leader's on the parent clan — and it's enforced server-side, so a request from a free account just won't go through. Hierarchy stays flat: a clan already acting as a parent can't also link in as someone else's child.

Once active, every linked clan shows up as a gold pill on your Dashboard — tap one to jump into the Clan Family screen, where rosters, war history, and CWL stats for the whole family are visible to any verified leader, regardless of their own Pro status. Either side can unlink at any time from the Family Clans list, no confirmation chain required beyond the prompt.
