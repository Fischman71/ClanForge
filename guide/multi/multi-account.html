Adding a second player tag

Got an alt, or lead more than one clan? Verify extra player tags from Settings → Accounts and switch with one tap — your Dashboard, members, wars, and AI tools all rescope to whichever tag is active.

Open Settings → Accounts → Add account. Look up the tag first — ClanForge shows the name, clan, and role before you commit — then Verify & Add runs the same one-time in-game API token check as your first launch. Free accounts can verify up to 2 tags; Pro raises that to 5.

Tip: Active and primary aren't the same thing. Active is just which account's view you're looking at. Pro status follows your primary — the first tag you ever verified — so adding or switching to an alt never touches your subscription.

Once you've added a second tag, a pill row appears above your Dashboard's clan header — tap a pill to flip the whole app to that account. Removing a tag asks for confirmation in the Accounts modal; if you remove your primary, the next-oldest verified tag is promoted automatically and your Pro grant moves with it.
